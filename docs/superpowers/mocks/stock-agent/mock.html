Agent settings: Flag, Ask me or Auto

One design

Your decision as a screen: a mode per change type, with per-client overrides. Alerts are always Flag. The buttons here switch.

What each mode means

Flag: posts the card. People make the change, and Birdy checks it on the next run.
Ask me: posts the card with Apply. Nothing changes until someone approves it.
Auto: makes the change within your guardrails, then posts what it did.
