Why has our close rate dropped this month?
Close rate fell from 25% to 18% while lead volume held steady, so this is a sales-side issue rather than a traffic one.

Three signals stand out:

1. Speed to lead slipped. Average first-call time went from 4m 12s to 11m 40s after the 6 Aug rota change. Leads called inside 5 minutes still close at 24%.

2. Price never came up. In 3 of the 5 lost calls I reviewed, no price was quoted before the call ended.

3. Afternoon coverage is thin. 41% of leads arrive between 2–5pm but only 2 agents are rostered.

The fastest fix is restoring the sub-5-minute callback rule — that alone should recover roughly 4 points of close rate.
