Onboarding · “Design your Slack report” step

The rest of onboarding follows Paper's wizard frame: Exit and segmented progress on top, a 560px column, and Back and Continue in the footer. The report copilot has no Paper equivalent. It is a chat with Birdy beside a live Slack preview. Here are three ways to fit it into that frame. The behaviour is the same in all three: chat drives the draft, the preview refetches, “Use this report” saves it, and “Skip, use the standard daily report” is still there.
