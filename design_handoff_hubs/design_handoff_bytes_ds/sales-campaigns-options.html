Sales Hub & Campaigns: pick one per widget

The ads gallery with its video preview, the single-call AI analysis and the agent batch analysis have no Paper equivalent. Each group below gives two or three ways to draw one with the new tokens. Data is sample UK clinic-agency data. Desktop on the left, 390px on the right.

3 · Member (agent) batch analysis

Call Centre → Members → sparkle on an agent. Analyses their latest calls, up to 50, in batches of 15. It shows a cost estimate before confirming, can run in the background, then summarises outcomes and lists a result per call.
